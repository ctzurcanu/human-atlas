# Duodenal gland detail: inventory and construction plan

The full applicable organ inventory is in TA98-DUODENUM-PARTS.md. A05.6.02.017 Glandulae duodenales is a descendant of A05.6.02.001 Duodenum in the local authority; its contextual occurrence belongs beneath Tela submucosa, A05.6.01.008, in both first wall hierarchies. Neither native model supplies registered gland geometry. A05.6.01.012 intestinal glands are mucosal crypts and must not share this mapping.

Construct an original branched tubular specimen rather than a whole-organ coat or duplicated crypt. A connected empty duct/secretory lumen is bounded by finite glandular tissue; terminal secretory branches are blind and the single outlet is open. The outlet annulus closes tissue thickness only and is an artificial specimen cut. The missing duct-to-crypt junction, submucosal stroma, muscularis mucosae crossing, lobular septa, cell types, vessels and actual proximal distribution are explicitly pending. This will be a shared magnified illustration for both sexes, not replacement anatomy in either body.

An implicit capsule tree defines outer and lumen fields. The glandular interval is the outer union minus the inner union, cut by one specimen mouth plane. A conforming tetrahedral grid extracts a single finite tissue boundary, joining branches without overlapping internal faces. Grid spacing, branch count, radius, wall thickness and specimen scale are original illustrative choices, not measured human morphology. Validate the entire extracted surface, connected components, oriented manifold edges, Euler characteristic, finite volume, open lumen and closed tissue mouth annulus, render and resource use before retaining it. No image textures, donor assets or redistribution of local sources.

Reference: [NUS pyloroduodenal histology](https://medicine.nus.edu.sg/ant/nushalo/eHistology/e/ef18/ef18.pdf) identifies Brunner glands in the submucosa and their alkaline mucus function, separately from mucosal crypts. Its images are monkey histology and are neither copied nor used as dimensional evidence here. The canonical TA98 parent remains unchanged.

## Withdrawn: inadequate anatomical evidence

The user correctly rejected the proposed shape. The cited NUS reference supports submucosal location and alkaline mucus function; it does not establish the capsule-tree morphology, branching topology, lobules or proportions shown here. Passing closed-surface, lumen, simplification and camera checks supplies computational evidence only. It does not prove anatomical fidelity.

The specimen is therefore rejected as a TA98 anatomical representation. Its main inspector and wall-review links have been removed. The direct diagnostic page is explicitly marked rejected, and original files remain for reproducibility. Both main gland memberships remain missing; no accepted coverage has been added. A replacement requires human histological/three-dimensional morphology evidence, explicit duct and secretory-unit topology, and reference-grounded rendered acceptance before reconnecting the hierarchy. Earlier implementation/simplification sections describe numerical trials, not anatomical acceptance.
