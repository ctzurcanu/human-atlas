# Female ocular surfaces for skin openings

Inventory before reconstruction. This local cosmetic/gross ocular assembly uses original low-resolution spheres guided by existing HRA scleral dimensions and the actual native eyelid UV footprints. It does not copy donor vertices, indices or textures. It provides the whole exterior only, not a solid-layer or microscopic reconstruction; named ocular layers, vessels, nerves and media remain independently staged or missing. Detailed eyes remain separate.

The actual eyelid openings are shader-discard regions. Eye centers fit beneath them; skin/head geometry stays unchanged. Optical-axis appearance is procedural white sclera/brown iris/dark pupil on the low-resolution globe, not accepted distinct TA98 coats or retinal features. No iris/retina exact mapping is inferred from color. Root radius 12.2 mm is the gross existing HRA scleral bounding scale, not an independently clinically calibrated measurement. Adjoining interfaces: native orbit, lids, head skin and optic-nerve course. Preserve native source geometry and camera. Existing HRA detailed eyes remain hidden.
