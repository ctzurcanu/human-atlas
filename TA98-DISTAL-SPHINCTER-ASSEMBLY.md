Primary reference abstracts consulted on 2026-10-07:

- *MRI anatomy of the anal region*, [PubMed 20940604](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20940604/): MRI in 30 women and 30 men distinguishes multiple muscular/fat-space layers and reports separate deep, superficial and subcutaneous bundles. Use cross-sections and surrounding spaces as references, rather than interpreting the current thin ring as complete coverage.
- *Magnetic resonance imaging and 3-dimensional analysis of external anal sphincter anatomy*, [PubMed 16319250](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16319250/): cross-sectional and reconstructed MRI observations provide a complementary subdivision reference. Its subdivisions must be reconciled with TA98 rather than equated to arbitrary source mesh pieces.

Full article retrieval was unavailable in the browsing session; only retrieved
primary abstract information is used here. Neither abstract establishes an
accepted scale or registration for our existing models. No article figures,
meshes or textures were copied.
