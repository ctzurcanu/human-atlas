This is a local male repair candidate, not installed or anatomically accepted. The full 61-term brachial-plexus inventory and source/neighbor scope remain in TA98-BRACHIAL-PLEXUS-REPAIR.md. No male source geometry is transferred to the female model.

A simple transverse fit was rendered and rejected because the surplus native proximal trunk formed a duplicate path and a visible neural loop alongside the donor roots. The revised candidate clips that surplus segment at a provisional, uncapped attachment boundary. It preserves both cords, both donor roots, the distant trunk, and the original distal attachment coordinates. The cut is an input boundary for complete junction reconstruction, not an accepted anatomical partition or a solid opening representation.

The geometric target is the mean of distal source-root vertex bands, not a clinically verified landmark. Maximum trial displacement is about 14.36 mm. The retained trunk has 4,908 triangles rather than 5,184. Both candidates have zero self-intersections. Independent saved-array validation proves complete parent-face lineage, exact distal boundary coordinates and exactly two boundary loops, with one replaced proximal join loop. No UVs, images or runtime assets are added.

The right candidate was inspected from front and back with both cords, both roots and native axillary vessels. Eight focused vessel checks preserve every original contact pair through face lineage. Existing brachial artery/vein crossings remain 104/108 per side; equal totals alone were not used as proof. Containment and the full surrounding closure remain unverified. Complete source union, non-overlapping named tissue partition, upstream source registration, biological nerve course and clinical shape acceptance are still required.

Primary anatomical references: [172-cadaver cord and median-nerve variation study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16795062/) and [median-nerve formation dissection study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35145795/). These studies show that origin levels and the relationship to axillary vessels vary; they do not establish this candidate's placement or dimensions.
