## Assembly interfaces

- Repair the entire source nerve junction before common/proper digital partitions; preserve distal twig and native course.
- Nerve, arterial wall, venous wall and bone tissue require disjoint volumes; triangle counts alone cannot establish them.
- Joint capsule is a covering, not a complete interphalangeal joint or collateral/palmar ligament.
- Generic vascular exteriors do not establish lumen, wall layers or source arterial ancestry.
- Skin mesh does not represent every epidermal/dermal layer, appendage or microscopic descendant.
- Do not move fixed skeleton/joint/tendon to compensate for a nerve defect.
- Retain all real anatomical openings; no blind caps or global welding.
- Verify full native neighbors/sections/attributes and bounded runtime resources before installation.
