Authority: the local TA98 extract and the complete small-intestinal inventories in TA98-DUODENUM-PARTS.md, TA98-JEJUNUM-PARTS.md and TA98-ILEUM-PARTS.md. This new original microscopic illustration does not replace any organ source or establish its regional distribution.

Assembly: one convex closed follicular envelope and its nested germinal-center envelope, sharing topology. The mantle occupies only the space between these boundaries; the core fills the inner boundary. Suppress the common coincident interface while both tissues are shown. A solitary view uses one secondary follicle; an aggregate view repeats the same shared carrier at six non-overlapping illustrative positions. Six is a display choice, not an anatomical follicle count. Dimensions, shapes, central symmetry and spacing are schematic. These are selected secondary follicles with germinal centers; primary follicles are not asserted.

Adjoining structures: intestinal mucosa, lamina propria and submucosa, follicle-associated epithelium, subepithelial dome, interfollicular T-cell regions, vessels and lymphatic collectors. These surrounding structures remain missing from this fragment. In particular, spaced follicles alone do not constitute a complete Peyer's patch. Actual organ placement, mucosal/submucosal extent, antimesenteric registration and sex-specific distribution remain missing. No fabricated solid opening, duct or whole-wall lymphoid layer.

Geometry gates: closed oriented edges, single connected genus-zero carrier/core, genus-zero outer and inner mantle boundary components, positive finite volumes, continuous paired-prism determinants and convex containment; no overlapping repeated envelopes. Render the actual assembled and sectioned tissues. Sections must fill solid core and mantle tissue separately, preserve the camera on selection/visibility/section input, and never draw the section guide. Shared position/normal/index arrays and materials across occurrences, no image textures, demand rendering. Keep main hierarchy memberships empty, adding only development links to the separate detail.

References: [human Peyer's patch immunohistochemistry](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3514387/) describes mantle zones, surrounding lymphocytes and dome/lamina-propria associations. [Organizing a mucosal defense](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16048539/) distinguishes organized solitary follicles/Peyer's patches from diffuse lymphocytes and mesenteric lymph nodes. The geometry parameters are not measurements from either reference.

## Local implementation and evidence

`app/intestinal-lymphoid-detail-geometry.ts` generates two corresponding 418-control convex envelopes and 832 carrier triangles. Actual packed latitude quadrilaterals choose their convex diagonal; the inner envelope uses a power-of-two homothety so the shared topology remains literally equivalent after float32 packing. Both displayed tissue cells reuse one position/normal bank. The mantle has two closed genus-zero boundary components; the solid center has one. There is no lumen or epithelial capsule. Outside dimensions are illustrative radii 0.48/0.38/0.34 mm; center dimensions are half those values, not clinical measurements.

The separate `/intestinal-lymphoid-review.html?mode=solitary` and `?mode=aggregate` viewer repeats the same geometry, index arrays and shader programs. Position, normal and stored tissue index arrays total 30,024 bytes, with 2,496 shared stored tissue triangles. A complete uncut solitary follicle draws 832 triangles after hiding coincident internal surfaces. Section rendering adds stencil counting and support geometry and is costlier than the stored triangle count. No images, source geometry or organ-coordinate edits. The viewer renders only on interaction; tissue selection, Shift-add selection, mantle/core visibility and live section input retain the absolute camera.

Measured complete aggregate section submission is 34,968 triangles, including all six occurrences, stencil counting and cap support quads. This is a separate magnified specimen cost, not an additional main-atlas burden or an FPS claim.

Three independent tests verify complete boundary edge orientation, connected components/Euler characteristics, positive tissue volumes, every packed point against every convex half-space, continuous paired-prism extrema, exact tissue containment versus empty cap support, nonoverlapping occurrence bounds, and actual male/female TA98 nodule links. All main nodule memberships stay empty; canonical/contextual inspector entries link the separate detail only. Existing crypt/villus tests pass, as do TypeScript, camera-intent, explosion-plane and explosion-viewport tests. The shared detail does not establish organ-level TA98 representation or accepted histological shape.

Actual browser review: aggregate sections show six separate solid germinal centers with surrounding mantle caps; hiding mantle changes cap count 12→6 and preserves the exact camera. Mantle/core cap clicks and Shift-add select both named tissues without moving the camera. Moving the section input and disabling it retain the exact view. The main male ileal Noduli lymphoidei aggregati entry exposes the detail link, also without camera movement. No shader errors were logged in the detail. Screenshots: outputs/intestinal-lymphoid-detail-section.jpg, intestinal-lymphoid-solitary-section.jpg, intestinal-lymphoid-hierarchy-link.jpg. Placement, dome/epithelial and interfollicular tissues, vascular and lymphatic routes and real follicular morphology remain pending; this is a schematic secondary-follicle illustration, not a completed Peyer's patch.
