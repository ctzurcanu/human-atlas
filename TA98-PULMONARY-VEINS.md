## Source-qualified lobar return

All 23 native male lobe material patches associate with the ipsilateral inferior vein for inferior lobes or superior vein for superior lobes and the right middle lobe. The middle-lobe route is typical, with alternatives explicitly stated. Reciprocal venous/drains links do not certify a numbered segment vein or complete perfusion territory, and do not make adjoining veins physical components.

Native female lungs have no qualified native pulmonary veins. Independently registered HRA veins remain excluded. Two inferior source node suffixes conflict with their FMA identities; no side reassignment follows from suffixes alone. Source geometry, materials and metadata stay unchanged.

[Pulmonary vascular anatomy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6039811/) supports typical lobar routes; [primary CT study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17236990/) documents middle-lobe drainage variants. [IFAA right inferior vein](https://ifaa.unifr.ch/Public/TNAEntryPage/auto/unit/LAEN/TAH4450%20Unit%20EN.htm) and [IFAA left inferior vein](https://ifaa.unifr.ch/Public/TNAEntryPage/auto/univ/EN/TAH4470%20Universal%20EN.htm) support FMA terminology without resolving donor registration.

No geometry/resources or accepted anatomy added. Source walls/lumens/ostia, whole native packing and missing tributaries remain unresolved.
