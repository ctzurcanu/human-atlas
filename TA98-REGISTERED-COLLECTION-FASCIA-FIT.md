# registered collection fascia fit: where the remaining misfit comes from (2026-11-16)

Geometry under review is `fascia-fit-v12`/`-v13` (`work/g_it4.npz`, 582,768 vertices, 1,840 parts). Nothing here changes installed geometry; anatomical acceptance stays false.

## What the bone-collision count really measures
The gate flag `inBone` was the winding test against every body bone *and* body cartilage mesh. 4,567 vertices were flagged. Of those:
- **1,762 (39%) are registered collection cartilage-class parts** (articular cartilage, discs, synostoses, menisci, labra) lying in the body's own cartilage-layer meshes (`… · Cartilage`, costal cartilages). That is one tissue represented twice, not a collision.
- **2,805 are true collisions**: median depth 0.19 mm, 90th percentile 0.26 mm, 99th percentile 0.52 mm; only 34 vertices are deeper than 0.5 mm (7 deeper than 1 mm, maximum 1.69 mm). They are mostly ligaments and capsules draped over bone and cartilage surfaces (radiate sternochondral, plantar calcaneonavicular, intercarpal, anterior longitudinal, medial palpebral).

The class-aware measure (`CLASSAWARE=1`, `scripts/lib.py: class_aware/excused`, used by `meas.py`, `finish.py`, `surgical.py`) excuses a cartilage-class vertex only when it is inside the full bone solid *and* its nearest obstacle surface is a body cartilage mesh. It can only remove flags (verified: 1,762 removed, 0 added; unset reproduces the previous flags bit for bit). The bone solids are closed only together with their cartilage cap meshes, so the inside test must keep the full surface; excluding cartilage faces opened the solids and made the count 17,955.

## Is the sternum registered wrongly? No.
For the sternochondral ligaments, costal cartilages and sternoclavicular parts the registered collection source already penetrates its *own* skeleton by 10–25% of its vertices (native frame), and the fit is similar or better for nearly all of them. The parts that got worse (xiphisternal synostosis, clavicular-facet cartilage, SC discs) are the cartilage-class duplicates excused above.

## Why the collisions are not cleared (corrected after measuring the body, 2026-11-16)
Body measurement (`scripts/body_overlap.py`, read-only; results `work/body_overlap.json`): of 2.245 m² of organ/muscle surface within 12 mm of bone, 0.131 m² (5.8%) lies inside a bone solid and 0.049 m² (2.2%) deeper than 0.5 mm. The deep overlap is concentrated: external abdominal oblique over ribs 9–10 (median 1.5 mm, max 3.8) and transversus abdominis over the hip bone and ribs (median 1.1 mm, max 6.7) account for about 290 cm² of the 489 cm²; gingiva over mandible/maxilla 48 cm²; pleura over T8–T9 16 cm²; supraspinatus, adductor brevis, gluteus minimus and teres major over their bones 5–10 cm² each. Muscle origins touch their bones, so part of this is attachment contact, but 1–7 mm is modelling error. Intercostals, transversus thoracis and the sternum are contact-level (median 0.1–0.15 mm), so the sternal region is not a body-overlap problem.

This does **not** explain the remaining fascia collisions. Of the 2,805 true bone-collision vertices only 13% are inside or within 0.5 mm of an organ (thorax 14%, head 13%, foot 14%, hand 0%); for the other 87% there is room (median 6.6 mm to the nearest organ surface) and they need only 0.6 mm of movement. Only the 34 deepest are mostly constrained (79% tight; back 72%: anterior longitudinal ligament, endothoracic fascia).

The broad push confirms it: the finish pass cleared the collisions geometrically (2,805 to under 120 vertices per iteration) but the no-regression gate then reverted 383 parts because moving them raised other measures (flips, creases, stretched or crushed edges, inside-organ), so the net gain was −2. The surgical push of the 34 deepest cost inside-organ +52 (thorax), flips +40, spikes +2 and creases for −41 bone collisions, and was also rejected. The limiting factor is the strict every-measure-no-worse rule on thin, already-stretched sheets, not the neighbouring meshes.

## Consequences
- Report 2,805 true bone collisions (class-aware) rather than 4,567; gate base `work/h0.npz` (geometry identical to g_it4); `work/h1.npz` passes the gate with −2 bone and −118 inside-organ, immaterial and not installed.
- Options: (a) keep the strict rule and accept the contact-level state, (b) a reviewed weighted gate that accepts a part when it removes more bone collisions than it adds flips/creases within stated caps, (c) resolve the body's abdominal-wall and gingiva overlaps first. (b) changes the acceptance rule and needs an explicit decision.

## Weighted gate trial (2026-11-16, option b)
`scripts/wgate.py` (copy of the gate with per-part acceptance), rules fixed before the first run. Per part, candidate vs base `h0`: hard (never worse) outSkin, inOrg, spike, attSum, attFar; required gain inBone down by at least 1; soft over2, under05, flip each at most +1 per 3 bone vertices removed; crease at most +1% of the part; region check soft increase at most 25% of the bone reduction; scope thorax and head_neck; everything else reverts to base.

Candidates: (1) the broad finish pass (class-aware, 0.8 mm margin) left only 35 in-scope parts with a net gain; 4 accepted, −31 bone. (2) A local push of every flagged vertex by depth + 0.4 mm with a 3-ring falloff (`scripts/surgical.py`, DEEP≈0) moved 20,332 vertices; 44 in-scope parts gained (−226 net across them), 6 accepted, −32 bone (head_neck −27, thorax −5), hard measures flat, region check pass.

Blocks on the 44 gaining parts: inOrg 29 (moving out of bone puts the vertex in muscle: no room), attSum 23, flip 19, crease 13, over2 6, under05 6, spike 5, attFar 2, outSkin 0. After the diagnostics I amended one rule (declared, `RULES=v2`): attSum may rise by 0.5 mm per bone vertex removed, because clearing a collision necessarily moves an attached edge off the bone by the clearance margin. The amendment unblocked none of the parts that remained blocked by inOrg, flips or creases: still 6 accepted.

Result: even allowing reviewed trade-offs, at most about 1% of the collisions (−32 of 2,805) can be removed locally without pushing fascia into muscle or degrading the mesh. Not installed (no v14). Seams are not the cause (only 7% of collision vertices lie on a cross-part seam). Remaining options: leave the contact-level state (median 0.19 mm) as is, or fix the body's own overlaps first.

## Opt-in body muscle correction (2026-11-16)
`scripts/muscle_fix.py` (workbench) writes `muscle-fit-v1/` (opt-in route `?model=local-ta98&fascia=registered-collection-review-muscles`; the ordinary routes and the base chunks are untouched, only positions are rewritten in copied chunks). Organ/muscle shells (same set as `ctx_f.py`) with vertices inside the full bone solid move along the way to the nearest bone surface by depth + 0.15 mm, with 3-ring falloff, welded vertices together, and a flip repair. A part is accepted only if flips ≤ max(2, 0.2% of faces) and deep (>0.05 mm) bone vertices fall by at least half. Result: 15,014 deep vertices → 2,814 in the accepted parts, 4 new flips. Rejected (unchanged): Gingiva (wraps the teeth), Bucinator, thyro-arytenoid, levator palpebrae, medial rectus, middle pharyngeal constrictor, multifidus thoracis, pectoralis minor and a few others (report.json).
Fascia re-fit against the corrected body: fascia inOrg 7,814 → 7,290, bone collisions unchanged (2,805); the surgical candidate plus the weighted gate then accepted 7 parts (−33 bone, strict caps) or 9 parts (−45, v3 caps). The fascia collisions are therefore not caused by body overlaps. No fascia re-fit installed; the layer carries fascia-fit-v13.

## Fat pad repair attempt (2026-11-16)
48 pads measured (`scripts/fatpads.py`): all open sheets (source design), no skin piercing, 6 bone vertices (left buccal), organ collisions mainly in the deep sub-orbicularis oculi and deep medial cheek pads (14-15 of 72 vertices), and left/right area differences of 23-37% in buccal, middle cheek and superior jowl pads. `scripts/pad_fix.py` pushed colliding pad vertices out (nearest-surface, then toward the skin up to 3 mm, falloff 2-6 rings, flip repair). The collision count did not converge (each pass flagged 70-116 vertices) because the thin pads are sandwiched between muscle, bone and skin: pushing out of one solid enters another or opens seams (95-104 opened). With the pad gain measure inOrg and bone, skin, spikes and attachments as never-worse, 0 of 5-6 gaining pads were accepted (blocked by flips and creases). Not installed.

### Install bug found and fixed (2026-11-17)
The first v14-v16 installs (`cC.py`) read unchanged vertices of partly moved parts from the stale chunk recorded in the gather metadata instead of the source atlas record, so 70 fascia parts (e.g. deltoid, pectoral, antebrachial fascia) were displaced by up to 5 cm in those builds. `cC.py` now reads the part's current record from the source atlas; `scripts/verify_install.py` compares an installed atlas against the measured gather (v16: 0 of 1,840 groups differ, max 1e-7 m), and a repository test (`v16 keeps v13 labels and every part within the repair distance`) fails if any part moves more than 6 mm (50 mm for the two mirrored pads). v14, v15 and muscle-fit-v1 were removed.
