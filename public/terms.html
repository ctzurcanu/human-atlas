Terms of use

Human Atlas is maintained by Christian Tzurcanu and provides interactive anatomy for general learning and reference.

Educational scope

Models are anatomical illustrations, not representations of a particular patient. Coverage, terminology and surface detail vary between sources, and some reference models are partial. The service does not provide diagnosis, treatment, medical-record access or interpretation of patient scans. Do not upload patient records or other sensitive personal information.
